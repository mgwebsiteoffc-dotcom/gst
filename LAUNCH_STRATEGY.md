# BikriBook: Launch & First Month Strategy (10-20 Clients)

This document outlines the tactical plan to onboard the first 10-20 paying clients for BikriBook within the first month.

## 1. Technical Setup (Done)
- [x] **Google Tag Manager (GTM)**: Integrated into all layouts. Ready for tracking conversions.
- [x] **Meta Pixel**: Integrated for Facebook/Instagram ad tracking.
- [x] **Affiliate/Partner Infrastructure**: Pages and application forms are live. Admin can manage payouts.

## 2. Partner & Affiliate Strategy (High Impact)
The fastest way to 20 clients is leveraging existing trust.

### A. The "Accountant/CA" Channel
CAs and Accountants manage GST for hundreds of SMEs.
- **Action**: Reach out to 50 local CAs. Offer them a free "Accountant Dashboard" and 20% recurring commission for every client they move to BikriBook.
- **Pitch**: "Stop chasing your clients for invoices. Let them use BikriBook, and you get automatic GSTR-1 data."

### B. Shopify Ecosystem
Since BikriBook has Shopify integration:
- **Action**: Identify Indian Shopify stores (use tools like BuiltWith or search "Shopify" in Indian FB groups).
- **Pitch**: "Automate your GST invoicing for Shopify. No more manual entry."

### C. Affiliate Program for Content Creators
- **Action**: Contact 10 "Fin-fluencers" on Instagram/YouTube who teach GST or business management.
- **Offer**: 20% recurring commission for 12 months.

## 3. Social Media Strategy
Focus on "Education" over "Selling".

### A. Instagram & Reels (Top of Funnel)
- **Content**: 3 Reels per week.
    - "3 GST mistakes small businesses make."
    - "How to generate an e-Way bill in 30 seconds."
    - "Why spreadsheets are killing your productivity."
- **CTA**: "Link in bio for a 14-day free trial."

### B. LinkedIn (B2B Authority)
- **Content**: 2 Posts per week.
    - Case studies: "How [Business X] saved 10 hours a week on billing."
    - Industry updates: "New GST changes for 2026."

## 4. Paid Ads Strategy (Low Budget, High Intent)
- **Google Search Ads**: Target high-intent keywords like "GST billing software India", "e-invoicing software", "Shopify GST app India".
- **Meta (FB/IG) Retargeting**: Show ads only to people who visited the website but didn't sign up.

## 5. The "First 10" Onboarding Tactic
- **Direct Outreach**: Reach out to your existing network.
- **Founder's Offer**: "Be one of our first 20 clients and get 50% off for the first year + free setup assistance."
- **Manual Success**: For the first 20 clients, do the data migration FOR them. This ensures they stick.

## 6. KPI Tracking
- **Signups**: Goal 100 free trials.
- **Conversion Rate**: Goal 10-20% (10-20 paying clients).
- **CAC (Cost Per Acquisition)**: Keep it below ₹1,500 for the Growth plan.
